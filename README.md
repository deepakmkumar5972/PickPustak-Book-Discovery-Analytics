# PickPustak: Book Discovery Platform & Website Analytics

## Project Overview

PickPustak is a digital book discovery platform developed as part of a Digital Innovation project. The platform was designed to help casual readers discover curated books, literary content, and reading recommendations through an engaging and user-friendly digital experience.

While the primary focus of the platform was book discovery and reader engagement, PickPustak also explored a secondary feature that allowed independent writers to publish exclusive content and reach a targeted audience.

The project combined digital product development with website analytics to understand user behavior, measure engagement, and evaluate the effectiveness of content discovery journeys.

The platform was developed using **Odoo Website Builder**, and website analytics were used to assess traffic patterns, user engagement, content performance, and audience demographics.

---

## Business Problem

With the abundance of online content and books available today, casual readers often struggle to discover quality literature that matches their interests. Most platforms focus heavily on transactions or large publishers, leaving limited room for curated literary discovery.

PickPustak was conceptualized to address this challenge by creating a platform that:

* Simplifies book discovery for readers
* Promotes curated literary recommendations
* Encourages reading through an intuitive digital experience
* Provides a dedicated space for independent writers to showcase original content
* Uses analytics-driven insights to continuously improve user engagement

---

## Project Objectives

* Develop a digital platform focused on book discovery and reader engagement
* Design intuitive content navigation and recommendation journeys
* Create a structured framework for website performance measurement
* Analyze visitor behavior and engagement patterns
* Evaluate content effectiveness using web analytics
* Generate recommendations to improve user retention and platform growth

## Analytics Focus

A comprehensive analytics framework was established to evaluate:

* Visitor acquisition and traffic trends
* User engagement and session quality
* Content popularity and page performance
* Reader navigation behavior
* Device and demographic insights
* Opportunities for improving content discovery and retention

The project demonstrated how digital product design and data analytics can be combined to improve user experience and drive engagement on a content-focused platform.
